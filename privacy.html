TrackDayIQ Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

In short:

  • Your sessions, recordings and notes stay on your phone.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We, the makers of TrackDayIQ, never receive your data. There is no TrackDayIQ server to send it to.
  • Data leaves your phone only when you choose to share a session, or when you ask the app to download an aerial image of a track.

Who we are

TrackDayIQ is a motorcycle track-day telemetry app, made by ronniegrove. Questions about this policy can be sent through github.com/ronniegrove.

What the app keeps, and where

Everything below is stored inside the app on your phone, and nowhere else:

  • Sessions: files you open from a data logger (such as AiM or RaceBox) or from another rider, and the analysis the app makes of them.
  • Phone recordings: when you record with the phone's GPS, its position, speed and heading once a second, from when you tap Start until you tap Stop.
  • Notes you write about a session, and a rider's name if you enter one for a session that is not yours.
  • Track images you add, and your settings, such as which traces and dash fields you prefer.

Deleting a session in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Location

The app uses your location only while you are recording a session with the phone. Recording continues with the screen locked and the phone in your pocket, so that a whole track session is captured; iOS shows the location indicator, and Android a notification, the whole time. The recording is saved on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

While you record at a known circuit, lap times and the gap to your best lap are shown on your lock screen. This is worked out on the phone and shown only on the phone.

Local network and Bluetooth

The app uses your phone's Wi-Fi connection to your own AiM data logger, and Bluetooth to your own RaceBox, to copy sessions from them to your phone. It can reach devices only on your local network, never the internet, and nothing is sent to them apart from the requests needed to copy your sessions.

When data leaves your phone

Only in these two cases, and only when you ask:

  • Sharing a session. When you tap Share or Export, the file goes wherever you send it through your phone's share sheet: another rider, a message, a folder. It contains the session's data, including GPS positions. If a recording started or ended away from the track, for example at home, those positions are in it too. Once you have sent it, what happens to that copy is up to its recipient.
  • Downloading an aerial image. If a track has no built-in image and you tap “Get aerial image”, the app asks the U.S. Geological Survey's public imagery service (nationalmap.gov) for a picture of the area around the track. That request contains the map area's coordinates, and, like any web request, your IP address. It contains nothing about your riding. The USGS's own privacy policy applies to that request.

The app makes no other network requests of its own. If you use the web version in a browser, the website that serves it receives the ordinary requests for the app's own files, as any website does; your sessions still stay in your browser.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or sell your data. We have no server that receives it.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not ask for an account, an email address or a phone number.

Backups

On iPhone, if you have turned on iCloud Backup or back up your phone to a computer, iOS includes the app's data in that backup, as it does for other apps. This is controlled by your phone's settings, not by the app. On Android, the app does not take part in device backups.

Beta testing

If you test TrackDayIQ through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with us, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. Crash reports are about the app, not your sessions.

Children

TrackDayIQ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Future features

If we ever add something that sends data off your phone, such as cloud storage or leaderboards, it will be off until you choose to turn it on, and this policy will be updated before it is released.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date, and describe the change in the app's release notes.
