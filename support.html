TrackDayIQ Support

Help with the app, and how to reach us

Contact: through github.com/ronniegrove.

Say which phone you use, the app's version (at the foot of Settings), and what you tapped and saw. If it is about one session, the logger it came from helps too. Do not send the session itself unless we ask.

Getting a session into the app

  • From a file. On the Sessions tab, under “Open a file”, choose a session: an AiM .xrk or .xrz, a Race Studio CSV, or a CSV from another logger.
  • From an AiM logger. Join the logger's own Wi-Fi network on your phone, then take sessions off it from the Sessions tab. The app asks for local network access for this.
  • From a RaceBox. Connect over Bluetooth from the Sessions tab and choose the rides to copy.
  • With the phone itself. Record from the Sessions tab. Allow location when asked; recording carries on with the screen locked.

Common questions

My file is grayed out or will not open. The app reads AiM .xrk and .xrz files and CSV files. It does not read Race Studio 2's .drk files: export the session as CSV from Race Studio instead.

The corners are numbered Turn 1, Turn 2… and those are not my circuit's numbers. At a circuit the app has no corner names for yet, turns are numbered in the order they come from the start line. The session's notes say when that is the case.

There is no aerial image under my lap. Tap “Get aerial image” on the Telemetry tab while you are online. It is fetched once and kept on your phone. Public imagery covers the continental United States only; elsewhere you can add an image of your own.

Lap times from my phone differ a little from my logger's. A phone fixes its position once a second, a logger ten times or more. Expect phone lap times to agree to within a few hundredths of a second, and corner detail to be coarser.

Where is my data? On your phone, and nowhere else. See the privacy policy.

How do I remove everything? Delete a session in the app to remove it; delete the app to remove all it stored.

Riding safely

TrackDayIQ describes what happened on track. It is not coaching and not a safety device. Do not operate the phone while riding.
